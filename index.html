Oikia — chores, bills and chat for shared homes

Where a place becomes a home.

One app for roommates, flatmates, couples and families: who does which chore, who paid for what, and what is happening this week — kept fair automatically.

Download on the App Store

What Oikia does

  • Chores that rotate fairlyWeighted by everyone's load and availability, so no one does more than their share.
  • Split bills and expensesRent, groceries and utilities, with an honest running balance of who owes whom.
  • Shared calendar and listsHouse events, shopping lists, house rules and documents in one place.
  • House chatA group chat for the whole house, plus direct messages.
  • Hestia, your house assistantAsk who is up next, swap a chore in plain language, or let her just do it.
  • RemindersGentle nudges so nothing gets forgotten — and no one has to nag.

How it works

  1. Create a house in Oikia — it takes a minute.
  2. Share the invite link with your housemates.
  3. Add your chores and bills. Oikia rotates them fairly and keeps the balance.

Who it is for

Roommates and flatmates sharing a rental, students in shared housing, couples moving in together, and families who want the house to run without the arguments.

Why "Oikia"?

Oikia (oh-KEE-ah) is the ancient Greek word for a household — not just the building, but the people, responsibilities and daily life shared within it. Oikia helps homes run with clarity, fairness and harmony.

Questions

Is Oikia free?

Yes. Oikia is free to download and use.

Does everyone in the house need the app?

Everyone who wants to take part does — chores and bills are shared between members. Invite them with the link from the Members screen.

Is it on Android?

Oikia is on iPhone today. Android is on the way.
