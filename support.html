Homie support

Homie fairly rotates household chores, handles swaps, and keeps an honest fairness record so housemates do not argue about whose turn it is.

Get help

Email liujianpingau@gmail.com and we will get back to you. Telling us what you were doing when it went wrong — and a screenshot — gets it fixed much faster.

You can also report a problem from inside the app: Settings → Contact support. That route attaches diagnostics automatically, so prefer it when you can.

Common questions

How do I join a house?

Ask a housemate for their invite code, then enter it on the join screen. Anyone already in the house can share it from the Members tab.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, chores, messages, and history. Houses you own pass to another member, or are removed if you are the only one.

Who owns a house?

Whoever created it, until they ask someone else to take over and that person accepts. Owners manage the invite code and can dissolve the house.
