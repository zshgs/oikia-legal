Oikia

Where a place becomes a home.

Oikia is the intelligent operating system for shared living. Organize chores, manage expenses, coordinate schedules, and communicate effortlessly — all in one place. Inspired by the ancient Greek oikia, where a household is more than a building, Oikia helps people build homes that run with clarity, fairness, and harmony.
